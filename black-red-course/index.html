FAQs

01

What happens during a discovery call?

A discovery call is a free 30-minute conversation where we talk about your goals, your challenges, and what you’d like to change. There’s no pressure to commit—this is simply a safe space to explore whether coaching feels right for you.

02

I don’t have a clear business idea yet

Many women begin with just a dream or a vague sense that they want more freedom and purpose. Coaching helps you clarify your idea, explore your strengths, and define a path forward—even if you’re not sure where to start.

03

Do I need prior business experience to benefit from coaching?

No. You don’t need an MBA or years of experience to start your own business. Coaching is designed to meet you where you are and give you practical tools, mindset shifts, and step-by-step support to get started.

04

What will we work on together?

We’ll focus on both mindset and strategy. That means building your confidence, and overcoming self-doubt, while also working on real business steps like defining your offer, identifying clients, and creating an action plan.

05

How long does it take to see results?

It depends on your goals, your pace, and how much time you can commit. Some clients make their first steps within weeks, while others work with me for several months to launch their business. We’ll design the process around your needs.

06

How do I get started?

Simply click Book a Free Call and choose a time that works for you. From there, we’ll connect on Zoom and begin your journey toward your dream business.
